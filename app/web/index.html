    What this system does

    1. Collects a corpus from a topic or seed papers.
    2. Extracts claims that must quote their evidence verbatim.
    3. Verifies each claim twice — the quote really occurs, and the claim actually follows from it.
    4. Maps where papers agree and disagree, and where the gaps are.
    5. Writes a review in which every sentence names the claims behind it, then checks each sentence and revises what fails.
